Prompt Engineering

Learn to craft powerful AI prompts from beginner to professional

Course Overview

This course covers the full spectrum of Prompt Engineering—enabling learners to harness large language models across industries with precision, strategy, and creativity.

  • Duration: 6–8 weeks
  • Format: Online, Live Workshops, Bootcamp, Corporate Training
  • Certification Included
